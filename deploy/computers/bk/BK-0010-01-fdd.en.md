# BK-0010-01 + KNGMD

A BK-0010-01 with the KNGMD unit (floppy disk controller) in the MPI bus connector. The
floppy controller is built on a K1801VP1-128; the ROM of the DISK 326 disk driver is at
address 0160000, and 16 KB of additional RAM at 0120000-0157777 take the place of the
first and second parts of BASIC.

Booting from a floppy is started with the directive

~~~
S160000
~~~

Drive 0 holds the ANDOS 3.30 floppy. Its splash screen waits for space or ВВОД (Enter),
then the shell starts.
